About MEWMO

MEWMO was founded by a university student. The long-term goal of the brand is to bring original home goods to overseas markets.

We operate with a light-asset model: independent design, cooperation with Chinese supply chains and overseas digital marketing.

We have completed brand naming, logo design, five original patterns, supplier communication, sampling and product photography. The brand is still in the market testing stage.

Our strengths are visual creation, product development and market research. Instead of competing with cheap generic storage bags, we take original design as our core advantage.

We serve both individual shoppers and B2B clients. We offer retail sales, as well as wholesale and custom services for small lifestyle shops, independent retailers and creators worldwide. We hope to build trust through retail and small cooperation projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these bags fully waterproof?

The laminated fabric resists water splashes, but we do not recommend long-time soaking in water.

Can I get custom patterns?

Yes, custom design is available for wholesale orders. MOQ and setup fees apply.

How long is the lead time for wholesale orders?

Lead time depends on order quantity. We will inform you after receiving your enquiry.

How is shipping cost calculated?

Shipping cost is calculated based on delivery address and order weight. Feel free to contact us.

Can the mini totes be folded?

Yes. All our bags can be folded for easy storage when not in use.
